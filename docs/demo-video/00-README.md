## Mistakes to avoid

1. **Don't record voice on the phone.** It picks up fan noise, lip plosives, and reverb.
   Record screen on the phone with **no audio**, then add voice over in OBS on a desktop.
2. **Don't read robotically.** Smile while you read — your tone changes audibly.
3. **Don't try to do it in one continuous take.** OBS lets you scrub the phone video while recording
   your voice; if you fluff a sentence, just pause, restart the video at the previous section
   boundary, and re-read. The script has natural 0.5s gaps between sections for exactly this.
4. **Don't forget to turn off notifications.** A WhatsApp banner at 1:32 will ruin the demo.
5. **Don't use Expo Go for the demo if you can avoid it.** The Expo Go shell is visually noisy.
   Build a debug APK from Emergent's Publish button and install it as a "real" app on the phone.
